01

Payment integration

6 items

The checkout is built on Stripe, which handles the card entry inside its own secure frame. This means card details never reach your website, and you carry no card-data liability. We need the account itself and the tax treatment to switch it on.

P1

A Stripe account on the selling entity

Blocking

Stripe must be opened in the name of the UAE entity that will actually receive the course fees, and activated to charge in AED.

  • Legal entity name, trade licence number and registered address
  • The UAE bank account that will receive payouts
  • Confirmation the account is activated for AED, not USD only

Why we need it Without a live account we cannot take a single payment. Please note Stripe does not onboard every UAE entity type — if your application is declined, tell us early and we will switch the checkout to a local gateway such as Telr, PayTabs or Network International. That change is roughly a day of work, but only if we know before launch.

P2

API keys and webhook access

Blocking

Once the account exists we need technical access to connect it. The safest way to give us this is to invite us to your Stripe dashboard as a developer, so you keep ownership and can revoke access at any time.

  • A developer invitation to the Stripe dashboard, or the publishable and secret keys
  • Permission to create a webhook endpoint on your domain

Please do not email the secret key

Sir, a Stripe secret key can move money and issue refunds. Please do not send it by email or WhatsApp. A dashboard invitation is safer and takes a minute; if you would rather send keys directly, we will set up a secure one-time link for you.

P3

Pricing confirmation

Before launch

We have built the pricing exactly as your brief describes it. Please confirm it is still correct, as the price is set by the website and cannot be altered by the buyer.

  • AED 2,000 for the first five places
  • AED 2,500 for the remaining places
  • A maximum of 15 participants in the cohort
  • Payment in full to secure a place, with no instalment option
P4

VAT treatment

Blocking

This needs to come from your accountant rather than from us. Your own brief requires the tax position to be shown to the buyer before they pay, and we cannot state it without your confirmation.

  • Is the selling entity registered for VAT?
  • Is AED 2,000 inclusive or exclusive of 5% VAT?
  • Does the treatment differ for participants outside the UAE?
  • What should appear on the buyer’s invoice — TRN, entity name, address?

Why we need it The checkout currently reads “VAT treatment confirmed at checkout”, which is a holding line. If VAT must be added on top rather than included, we enable Stripe Tax and adjust the displayed price — a small change, but the wording has to match the reality.

P5

Refund and cancellation terms

Blocking

The site presently shows a placeholder: a full refund if a participant withdraws more than seven days before the start date. This is our assumption, not your policy.

  • The notice period and the amount refunded
  • The position once the course has started and materials have been accessed
  • What participants receive if you cancel or reschedule the cohort

Why we need it This figure appears in three places — the checkout, the FAQs and the refund policy — and all three must agree. It is shown to the buyer before payment, so it has to be your real policy.

P6

Before launch

Why we need it An unrecognisable name on a card statement is the most common cause of a chargeback. It is a small setting that prevents a real problem.

Legal policies

3 items

Your brief instructed us to build the page structure but not to invent the policies, and we have followed that precisely. Five pages exist with the correct section headings, each carrying a visible notice that it is not in force, and each hidden from search engines. They will remain that way until you supply approved wording.

L1

Five documents, reviewed and approved

Blocking

We have prepared an outline for each, listing the sections it needs to cover. Your adviser can write directly against those headings.

  • Privacy policy — what is collected, lawful basis, the processors used (Stripe, email platform, video host, analytics), international transfers, retention, and individual rights
  • Cookie notice — every cookie set, its purpose and duration, and how consent is managed
  • Course terms of sale — what is supplied, price and tax, scope of the service, rescheduling rights, intellectual property, recording and confidentiality, liability, governing law

This may be quicker than it looks independentadvisors.ai already carries a privacy policy, cookie policy, terms and conditions, copyright policy and disclaimer. If the same adviser drafted those, adapting them for Cash Flow Mastery will be considerably faster than starting again — the privacy and cookie notices in particular need little more than the new processors added (Stripe, the email platform, the video host and analytics). The terms of sale is the one genuinely new document, because Independent Advisors sells advisory services rather than a fixed-price course with a refund window.

Hosting decision

Blocking

One technical decision that needs your approval because it carries a small monthly cost.
