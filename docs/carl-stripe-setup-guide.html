Payments · Stripe setup guide

Connecting payments: your Stripe setup, step by step

Everything needed to start taking course payments in UAE Dirhams: what to have ready, the steps in your Stripe account, what we handle for you, and the decisions we need from you.

Dear Sir,

The payment flow on the Cash Flow Mastery website is built and tested. It takes payment in AED, confirms each place automatically, stops the cohort at its capacity, and withdraws a place if a payment is fully refunded. What it is waiting for is a Stripe account in your business’s name.

Stripe has to open that account for you directly, because it verifies the business and pays the money into your bank account. This guide keeps your part as short as possible: you set up and verify the account, then invite us in. We do all of the technical connection and testing from there.

One principle runs through this guide: please never send us Stripe keys or passwords by email or WhatsApp. Inviting us as a team member (step 6) means no secret ever needs to be copied or shared.

01

Have these ready first

Before you sign up

Stripe asks for these during activation. Having them to hand means the whole sign-up can be done in one sitting. Stripe only accepts colour copies in PDF, JPEG or PNG format — screenshots are rejected.

  • UAE trade licence or freelancer permit Stripe requires a licence or permit issued within the UAE to open an account.
  • If the business is an LLC: memorandum of association The document (or equivalent) showing the business owners and their share split. If a holding company owns 25% or more, its memorandum is needed too.
  • If the business is an LLC: a recent bank statement Proof of an active business bank account, dated within the last six months.
  • Identity documents for you and every owner of 25% or more UAE and GCC nationals: passport and Emirates ID. Other nationalities: passport, Emirates ID and residence visa.
  • A UAE bank account (IBAN) that accepts AED This is where course payments are paid out.
  • Power of Attorney — only if needed If the person setting up the account is not named as an owner or manager on the licence documents, Stripe needs a Power of Attorney for them, dated within the last three months.
  • Business details Registered business name and address, a business phone number, and the website address for the course.
02

Your steps in Stripe

7 steps

Please follow these in order. Most take a few minutes; the activation form in step 3 is the longest.

You · stripe.com

Create the Stripe account

  1. Go to dashboard.stripe.com/register.
  2. Sign up with a business email address you will keep long term.
  3. Choose United Arab Emirates as the country. This cannot be changed later.

You · 2 minutes

Turn on two-step sign-in

In your Stripe profile settings, switch on two-step authentication, using an authenticator app or text message. This account will receive real money, so a password alone is not enough.

You · the longest step

Activate payments

In the Dashboard, select Activate payments and complete the form:

  1. Choose the business type that matches your trade licence.
  2. Enter the business details and upload the documents from section 01.
  3. Add yourself as representative, and every owner of 25% or more.
  4. For the business description, you can use: “Online four-week cash-flow training programme for small business owners, sold through our website.”

Stripe then reviews the account. Once you have invited us (step 6), we can build and test while the review runs.

You · during activation

Add the bank account for payouts

Add your UAE business bank account (IBAN) in AED. Stripe pays out course payments five business days after each payment, and payouts can be delayed by UAE public holidays.

You · required for checkout to work

Complete your public business details

Open dashboard.stripe.com/settings/public and fill in:

  • Terms of service URL — this is essential. Our checkout asks buyers to accept your terms, and Stripe will not allow that until this link is set.
  • Privacy policy URL.
  • Support email and phone number that buyers will see.
  • Statement descriptor — the name shown on a buyer’s card statement, for example CASHFLOWMASTERY. A recognisable name here prevents disputes from buyers who do not recognise the charge.

We will give you the final page addresses for the terms and privacy links once the policy wording is approved (see decision D5).

You · 2 minutes

Invite Flarenet as a Developer

  1. Open Settings, then Team and security.
  2. Invite the Flarenet email address we send you.
  3. Select the Developer role only — not Administrator.

The Developer role lets us create the connection keys, set up payment notifications and test everything. It cannot change your bank account or payout schedule, and cannot invite anyone else. It can view payments and issue refunds, which we will only do for test payments or on your written instruction. You can remove our access at any time.

You · one message

Tell us it is done

Send us a short message once the invitation is sent. No keys, no passwords, no screenshots of settings — we will have what we need through the invitation.

Please do not

Send Stripe secret keys, passwords or verification codes by email, WhatsApp or any chat. Anyone with a secret key can create charges and refunds on your account. The invitation in step 6 makes sharing them unnecessary.

03

What we handle once invited

8 tasks

None of this needs your time, except the single real payment at the end.

F1

Create restricted connection keys

Flarenet

Keys limited to only what the checkout needs, stored solely in the website’s secure hosting settings — never in email, chat or the code itself.

F2

Connect payment notifications

Flarenet

Stripe tells the website instantly when a payment succeeds, fails, expires or is refunded, so places are confirmed and withdrawn automatically. Each notification is verified with a signature so it cannot be faked.

F3

Test the full flow in Stripe’s test mode

Flarenet

Using Stripe’s test cards: a successful payment, a declined card, a card needing extra bank authentication, a full refund, and a sold-out cohort. No real money moves.

F4

Configure checkout and receipts

Flarenet

Show your terms, privacy policy and support details at checkout, and turn on emailed receipts for buyers.

F5

Set up fraud protection

Flarenet

Review Stripe Radar, Stripe’s built-in fraud screening, and apply sensible rules for a course priced at AED 2,000–2,500.

F6

Switch to live payments

Flarenet

Once Stripe approves your account, replace the test keys with live keys and repeat the key checks.

F7

One real payment, then a refund

With you

You make one genuine purchase on the live site; we confirm the place was recorded, then refund it in full and confirm the place was released.

F8

Handover

Flarenet

A short guide to finding payments, issuing refunds and reading payouts in your Dashboard — and removal of our Developer access afterwards, unless you prefer us to keep it for support.

Where your money and data sit

Card details are entered directly into Stripe’s secure form on the checkout page and never touch the website or our systems. Payments settle into your Stripe balance and are paid out to your own bank account. Flarenet never has access to the funds, and cannot change where they are paid.
