On accessibility, since your brief asked for it specifically

We audited every page programmatically rather than by eye. Text and background colour were measured on each element against the WCAG AA standard: 187 items of text on the home page and 276 on the sales page, with no failures. Every page has one main heading, no skipped heading levels, labelled form fields, and full keyboard navigation.

Two brand colours had to be darkened slightly to reach the standard at small sizes. The change is imperceptible beside the Independent Advisors site and is documented in the handover.

Your design instructions were unusually specific, and they shaped the site more than any other section of the brief.

§10

What you ruled out, and what we did instead

Honoured

No fabricated testimonials, results or client logos. No countdown timers, scarcity devices or guarantees. No institutional finance imagery and no generic stock-market visuals. No fear-based messaging.

That removes almost every device a course landing page normally leans on, so the visual interest comes from the subject itself: an interactive 13-week cash forecast the visitor can drive with three sliders, and the operating cash cycle drawn to scale. Both are labelled as illustrations, not as advice.

05

Learner area — your brief §7 and §9

Held, by your instruction

Why this is not built yet

Your brief §9 says plainly: “Do not custom-build authentication, video streaming, payments or course progress merely for the founding cohort.” We have followed that.

For fifteen participants, lessons and templates can be delivered by email with video on a hosted platform, and the live sessions run on Zoom or Meet. That gets the cohort running without paying to build a learning platform before anyone has bought a place.

The route is reserved and the groundwork is done, so building it later does not mean rebuilding anything. Both options — including setup cost, monthly cost, maintenance responsibility, data ownership and migration implications — are set out in the technical handover.
