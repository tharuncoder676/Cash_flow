
Cash Flow Mastery — Website and Mini-Course Developer Brief
Website: www.cashflowmastery.co
Product: Cash Flow Mastery founding mini-course

1. Objective
Configure the website to sell and deliver a four-week blended Cash Flow Mastery programme for SME owners and managing directors. The learner should be able to discover the offer, understand the outcome, pay, receive onboarding, access short lessons and resources, join weekly live sessions, track progress and complete a practical 13-week cash-flow forecast.
The initial site must support a paid founding cohort without becoming a costly custom-learning platform. Prefer reliable existing components and integrations over bespoke development.
2. Core audience and promise
Audience
Owner-led SMEs, initially in the UAE and GCC, whose owners are profitable or growing but lack reliable cash visibility. They may feel that money is constantly tight, despite apparently healthy sales or profits.
Core promise
Understand where your cash is going, see cash problems 13 weeks ahead, and leave with a practical cash action plan.
Brand belief
Finance is the language of business. Owners do not need to become accountants, but they should understand what their business is telling them.
3. Product structure
Format
Four-week blended founding cohort.
Short self-paced lessons released by week or available from the start, depending on platform practicality.
One live group application session with Carl each week.
Downloadable diagnostic, worksheets, 13-week forecast template and implementation plan.
10–15 founding participants.
AED 2,000 for the first five places and AED 2,500 for the remaining places.
Payment in full to secure a place.
Live sessions provide education and group application, not unlimited company-specific CFO advice.

9. Platform principle
Use the existing website stack where it can meet the requirements reliably. If an LMS is needed, integrate a proven hosted platform or established LMS/member component. Do not custom-build authentication, video streaming, payments or course progress merely for the founding cohort.
The developer should provide two options:
Fast founding-cohort build: minimum viable pages, checkout and learner area by the launch dates.
Scalable phase-two architecture: the upgrades required after demand and learner outcomes are proven.
For each option, state setup cost, recurring monthly cost, transaction fees, implementation time, maintenance responsibility, data ownership, integration limits and exit/migration implications.
10. Content and design requirements
Reuse the existing Cash Flow Mastery brand where appropriate, but improve clarity and consistency.
Tone: friendly, practical, experienced, plain English and confidence-building.
Avoid institutional finance imagery, fear-based messaging and generic stock-market visuals.
Use clear cash-flow diagrams, screenshots of tools and practical SME situations.
CapCut Pro is the approved video editing platform.
Video production will favour edited existing footage; only a short new welcome and closing are planned unless technical corrections are necessary.

12. Acceptance tests
Before release, confirm that a test user can:
Find and understand the course offer.
Register and pay the correct amount.
Receive confirmation and login instructions.
Access only the purchased course.
Watch captioned video on desktop and mobile.
Download the correct resources.
Mark lessons complete and resume progress.
Find the next live-session details.
Reset a password and contact support.
Complete the feedback/diagnostic forms.
Confirm that analytics record the lead, checkout and purchase events and that failed payments do not create active course access.
